### Steps to Get Up and Running;

- **Step 1:** Create a Regular Microsite Document (do all the setup required, header/footer/meta...)
- **Step 2:** Open Uncategorized Tours Tab,

  - **Step 2.1:** Create a `Category` Slice
  - **Step 2.2:** Add the Headout Category ID, additionally you can specify `excluded tgids`, `no of tours`, `CSV tour ranking`

  > If you Require to override any tgid, in Repeatable Zone Specify the TGID of the tour and add in the details that needs to be overridden.
